<Lede>
Heymarket is a San Francisco SaaS company for business text messaging. I joined as the first go-to-market engineering hire while finishing my senior year, with a brief to make outbound systematic instead of heroic.
</Lede>

## The situation

Sales had good instincts and a lot of manual work. Enrichment, list building, segmentation, and personalization all happened by hand, which meant they happened inconsistently.

## What I built

- **Automated outbound workflows in Clay and Outreach.** Enrichment, segmentation, and personalized messaging across the priority customer groups, so a rep started each week with a qualified, researched list instead of a blank one.
- **Lead scoring** that combined firmographic, technographic, and behavioral data. Sales got a ranked view of who to call and a shared definition of why.
- **A/B tests** across messaging, channels, and personas, with feedback loops from Sales and Product folded back into targeting and positioning.

## What I took from it

Working next to a mature sales team taught me what a system has to do to be adopted: it has to save a rep time on Monday morning, or it is shelfware. That lens shaped everything I built afterwards.
