<Lede>
Innovo Markets is a Chicago fintech building infrastructure for energy commodities. I came in to help an early sales motion get cleaner data and a more consistent way to reach the market. I left having also built a bridge between the company and my own engineering department.
</Lede>

## The commercial work

- Built and tuned ZoomInfo-to-HubSpot workflows for lead enrichment, ICP segmentation, and sales tracking.
- Cleaned and standardized 500+ accounts and contacts so outbound stopped bouncing off bad data.
- Developed targeted lead-generation and outbound workflows using AI tools and account research.
- Produced four UAE market-entry decks for partner and investor conversations, plus sales collateral including a demo storyboard and a LinkedIn carousel set.

## The bridge

After the summer, I approached Innovo's CEO and connected him with the Industrial and Enterprise Systems Engineering department at UIUC. Innovo became a formal sponsor of ISE Senior Design, a relationship that did not exist before and that no course required me to create.

I then led the capstone team. We aligned Innovo's real operational questions with the academic brief and ran Monte Carlo simulations to develop the company's pricing strategy. The capstone advisor described the project as one of a kind. Innovo remains a sponsor, so students after me get a real industry problem to work on.

## What I took from it

The most valuable things I did that year were not on the job description. Seeing a gap between two institutions I belonged to and simply closing it is the pattern I want to repeat.
