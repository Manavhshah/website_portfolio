<Lede>
Integrow is a Mumbai asset manager that was evaluating a move into portfolio management services for real estate, in a market where alternative investment funds were getting crowded. I was asked to help figure out whether and how to enter.
</Lede>

## What I did

- **Market analysis.** Benchmarked competitors and built an attribute heat map to find where existing products were thin.
- **Financial modeling.** Modeled debt products, including collateralized bond structures and default-risk scenario simulations, in Excel and Python.
- **Go-to-market.** Delivered version one of the product thesis for what became India's first real-estate-based PMS, and briefed leadership on next-phase priorities.
- **Automation.** Rebuilt recruitment and alerting workflows on the internal HR platform, cutting cycle time by roughly 150%.
- Shadowed the CEO across investor relations and strategic planning.

## What I took from it

This was the first time I got to combine engineering habits with a commercial question, and the first time I saw a thesis I wrote turn into something a firm actually launched.
