<Lede>
Mag Mile Capital is a Chicago mortgage banking firm. I spent the summer between underwriting models and a software pilot, which turned out to be the first time I saw what happens when you put AI tooling in front of a team with real deals on the line.
</Lede>

## What I did

- Applied financial models to support underwriting of $70M+ in hospitality and multifamily real estate transactions.
- Led project coordination for testing and implementing AI-based underwriting software: aligning engineering, product, and the investment team on workflow fit.
- Built industry and lender research databases to support deal origination.
- Prepared client-facing materials: market overviews, financial summaries, and loan request packages.

## What I took from it

A model is only as useful as the review process around it. The pilot taught me to design for the people who have to trust the output, not just the people who build it.
